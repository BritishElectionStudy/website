# Required partner and funder logos

Retrieved 4 November 2026 for the preliminary BES templates. Use all four on report
front covers and presentation title slides, in a full-width white footer band.
The order is Royal Holloway, Manchester, King's, ESRC. Retain original colours,
proportions and clear space. Do not recolour these marks into the BES palette.

| Mark | Asset and source |
|---|---|
| Royal Holloway University of London | `royal-holloway.jpg`, 945 × 472; [official logo guidance](https://www.royalholloway.ac.uk/about-us/our-brand/logo/) and [direct artwork](https://www.royalholloway.ac.uk/media/ubxnw4rb/logo-large-london.jpg). The report uses a width of 45 mm, matching the A4 recommendation. |
| The University of Manchester | `university-of-manchester.png` and original `.eps`, from the [official MSA identity pack](https://www.msa.ac.uk/identity/) ([ZIP](https://www.msa.ac.uk/media/msaacuk/documents/identity/msa-logos-pack-2025.zip)), `Partner Universities/University of Manchester/Tab_logo/White backgrounds/TAB_col_white_background`. [University guidance](https://www.staffnet.manchester.ac.uk/brand/visual-identity/logo/). |
| King's College London | Original `kings-college-london.svg` from the [official website asset](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/SiteElements/2017/images/kcl-logo.svg); PNG is an unchanged raster rendering. [King's image library](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/library/collections/image-library). |
| ESRC / UKRI | `esrc.png`, 490 × 380, from the [ESRC council page](https://www.ukri.org/councils/esrc/) ([direct artwork](https://www.ukri.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ukri-esrc-square-logo.png)). [UKRI brand hub](https://ukri.frontify.com/d/zgfuBB2r7aAg/brand-basics). |

Artwork rights remain with the respective institutions. This kit uses official-site
artwork for the user's stated BES attribution requirement. The raster files are
adequate for these prototype footer sizes; obtain print masters from the brand
teams for final commercial production. The guide does not replace their brand rules.
